# Pro reports

* **Show Geometry Quality**: Clockwise rings, duplicate points, excess precision, empty geometries and bounding boxes that do not fit.
* **Show Measurements**: The length and area of every line and polygon, with totals.

The first Pro command starts a free 14-day trial on this computer. After the trial, Pro is a one-time purchase: USD 19 (Personal) or USD 39 per user (Business).
